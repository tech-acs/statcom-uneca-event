Background

The Statistical Commission for Africa is the regional equivalent of the Statistical Commission of the Economic and Social Council, which is the highest body in the global statistical system. It is the highest decision-making body in Africa responsible for setting statistical standards, developing concepts and methods and implementing them at the national, subregional and continental levels.

target

Objectives

The meeting provides an opportunity for participants to discuss the link between African and global statistical systems to ensure that Africa is not left behind in global statistical initiatives.

  • check_circle Discuss progress and statistical development in African statistical systems.
  • check_circle Take stock of ongoing global initiatives and mainstream new methodologies.
  • check_circle Modernize and transform national statistical systems in Africa.
flag

Expected Outcomes

  • done_all Endorsement of the revised African Charter on Statistics.
  • done_all Commitments to increase domestic financing for statistical systems.
  • done_all Adoption of guidelines for the 2030 round of Population and Housing Censuses.
